Sort It Out ♻️

Clean up the room before time runs out!

How to play

  1. Point your phone at the floor and tap to place the bins.
  2. Find the trash around you. Some of it is behind you!
  3. Tap trash to pick it up, aim at the right bin and swipe up to throw.

Swiss recycling rules

  • PETdrink bottles only, in any colour
  • ALUcans, trays and foil
  • GLASSsorted by colour: clear, brown, green. Other colours go into green.
  • PAPERnewspapers, magazines, office paper
  • TRASHthings that look recyclable but aren't

⚠️ Watch your surroundings while you play.
